# Worked concept examples — illustrative, not outcome claims

## ASAN / astronaut
Palette: Space Navy `#081525` (base), Soul Silver `#DCE7F0` (secondary), Cosmic Copper / Solar Orange `#FF6A00` (accent; artistic copper name, visibly vivid orange).
Master scene: helmeted astronaut front and centre, Earth horizon, nebula, enormous ORBIT title behind subject. Outline only intersecting typography on the visor in a controlled overlay; maintain contrast. CTA: “Become an ASANAUT”.
A: monochrome navy. B: silver adds typography and cool rim-light, no orange. C: orange nebula, orbital path, visor reflection and CTA illuminate.
Example VO: “Space Navy and Soul Silver. Quiet. Add Cosmic Copper, and now we’re interstellar.”

## VÉRITÉ / luxury fragrance
Palette: Deep Teal `#071C25` (base), French Rosé `#F2A7C3` (secondary), Electric Lime `#B8FF3C` (accent).
Master scene: dramatic tilted faceted perfume bottle, shallow rose/teal studio lighting, big elegant serif title, a single shop CTA. Bottle label can read VÉRITÉ; keep it distinct from unrelated nav branding.
A: deep teal, no lime. B: rose glass and elegant typography. C: lime reflections and ribbon of light activate; preserve bottle geometry.
Example VO: “Deep Teal and French Rosé. Sophisticated. Add Electric Lime, and now it’s a party.”

## Yamaha R1 / performance motorcycle
Palette: Obsidian Black `#050711` (base), Electric Blue `#1648FF` (secondary), Ultraviolet `#C13CFF` (accent).
Master scene: close-up of moving sportbike, wheel/visor/fairing in frame, oversized R1 typographic outline intersecting the subject. Retain R1 marking on the bike when requested.
A: high-contrast dark bike. B: vivid blue accents and type. C: violet light streaks and reflections cut across motion-blurred background; keep bike fixed.
Example VO: “Obsidian Black and Electric Blue. Fast. Add Ultraviolet, and now it’s electrifying.”

These palettes and scripts are creative examples; swap names, colours and subject while preserving the underlying workflow.
